The Best Movies According to Ruhul

My top 5 movies of all-time.

The Godfather

The gold standard for crime dramas — layered characters, unforgettable dialogue, and near-perfect direction.

The Dark Knight

Elevated superhero films into serious modern epics. Heath Ledger’s Joker is legendary.

The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King

Massive emotional payoff and one of the greatest fantasy achievements ever filmed.

Parasite

Brilliant social commentary mixed with suspense, humor, and genre-bending storytelling.

Interstellar

Emotional, ambitious sci-fi with unforgettable visuals and music.
